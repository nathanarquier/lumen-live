Terms of Use & Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Lumen is an early-stage, pre-launch product built by Nathan Arquier. It's still being tested with a small number of users. This page explains what the service is, what happens to your data, and how to stop using it if you want to.

1. What Lumen is

Lumen is a chat-based coaching tool for early-career musicians. You're not paying for it, there's no guarantee it stays online, and features may change or break while it's in this stage. It's intended for users aged 18 and over.

2. What data we collect and why

  • Your email address and login, to identify your account (handled by Supabase, our authentication and database provider).
  • The messages you send in chat, and Lumen's replies. Your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response; that's the only way the coaching function works.
  • Information Lumen infers from your conversation (your stated goal, obstacle, career stage) and the missions you're offered or complete, so the app can pick up where you left off.
  • Basic usage events (e.g. that a mission was offered or completed) so we can tell whether the product is actually helping anyone. Not sold or shared, used only to improve Lumen.

We don't use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. Your session sign-in is stored locally in your own browser, not shared with anyone else.

3. Who we share it with

Only the two processors above: Supabase (storage and authentication) and Anthropic (to generate chat responses). We don't sell data, and we don't share it with advertisers or other third parties.

4. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the sidebar. Once you do:

  • Your account is scheduled for permanent deletion in 30 days.
  • If you log back in before then, you'll be offered the choice to restore it.
  • After 30 days, your account and all associated data (conversations, profile, missions, usage events) are permanently and automatically deleted. This can't be undone.

5. Your rights

You can ask what data we hold on you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it sooner than the 30-day window, by emailing lumenmusic.app@gmail.com.

6. Changes

Because Lumen is still being built, this page may change as the product does. If it changes in a way that matters, we'll flag it in-app.
